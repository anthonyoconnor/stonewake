# Standalone geography briefs

M42 composition plans. Each map receives an individual concept before its replacement is authored. The [level overhaul](../level-overhaul.md) governs acceptance. Stable catalog IDs and the complete independent starting catalog remain intact; these are paid settlement games, with no researched spells or owned service rooms on arrival.

| Stable ID | Proposed identity and scale | Useful geography and choices |
|---|---|---|
| `border-foothold` | Mining interchange, 48 × 40 | A central Hearth refuge feeds several old mine branches. Northern watch route competes with a west service loop; an optional southeastern gallery provides additional income and a physically suppressible entrance. Timber mine architecture and a damp abandoned bay distinguish this hub from the campaign's protected peripheral basin. |
| `emberwater-crossing` | Water-and-fire confluence, 48 × 40 | An irregular cold-water channel and molten branch separate three substantial banks. The short double crossing approaches the relay directly; a wider southern crossing reaches useful foundry services. Shore peninsulas determine safe consolidation space. The extra area expands banks and districts rather than stretching two straight hazard stripes. |
| `region-upper` | Honeycomb quarry workings, 46 × 38 | Several broad extraction chambers weave around thick rock ribs. East-side start, northwest relay, a short breached working face versus an older timber service circuit. Mineral pockets follow the ribs; an optional lower cave holds more demanding pressure and income. No second floor height or literal terraces. |
| `region-fungal` | Overgrown confluence, 50 × 42 | A southern refuge approaches three wet cavern lobes through branching dry necks. A buried masonry crossing and overgrown waystation form a central settlement opportunity; the two approaches pass different colony margins. Reuse fungal pools with an ancient civic fragment instead of reproducing the campaign's single peripheral basin loop. |
| `region-ancient` | Flooded watch districts, 54 × 44 | Two broken civic courts face each other across water, with a dry ruined service street around one shore and a paid causeway across the other. Northwest arrival and southeast objective. Reclaimable district walls, kitchens and workshops give reasons to choose a bank and establish remote support. |
| `region-crystal` | Prism wells, 54 × 44 | Central refuge in a radial cave junction; fractured chambers, a southern chasm recess and two optional mineral branches. The northeast relay has a short bare approach and a longer archive loop. The central start and spoke decisions distinguish it from the campaign's east-to-west crescent expedition. |
| `region-volcanic` | Ashen caldera, 60 × 48 | A broken lava ring surrounds a ruined citadel. West-bank start, objective inside the ring, and two radial crossing sites with different exposed shore/foundry rewards. Optional outer-bank income and a hostile feeder passage create a complete expedition without requiring the whole ring to be cleared. |

These dimensions are provisional until normal-route and visual review. Each new definition needs its own safe finite seam shapes, deliberately gated discoveries, example support/route/defense positions, optional income decision and readable final objective. A shared settlement budget does not justify repeating the same resource stripes. Example routes may buy additional Stonehands or forward rooms through existing actions if the larger scenario warrants it; normal paid actions must remain visible in evidence.
