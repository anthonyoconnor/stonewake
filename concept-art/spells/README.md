# Spell concept and model references

These nine sheets were generated and inspected on 2026-09-09 for the user-authorized spell graphics overhaul. Each current spell lacked a dedicated effect sheet. The existing [Stonehand v2](../stonehands/stonehands-v2.png) remains the accepted character design; the new Create Stonehand sheet specifies only its assembly magic.

All images were produced with the built-in image generation tool and are retained in this directory. Exact prompts and provenance are in [primary prompts](prompts-primary.md) and [secondary prompts](prompts-secondary.md). They are visual references; the renderer uses authored Babylon.js meshes rather than these images as textures.

| Spell / concept | Starting appearance | Refined design and iteration |
|---|---|---|
| [Create Stonehand](summon-stonehand-v1.png) | No spell geometry or assembly burst. The existing worker was created directly. | Octagonal bronze forge seal, eight recessed rune sockets, inner amber channels, orbiting stone/bronze fragments, open diamond core lattice and short-lived sparks. It surrounds the actual worker model and fits the existing 0.7-second burst lifetime. |
| [Haste](dwarf-haste-v1.png) | Twelve gold ground squares. | Six angular ground rune segments, three rotating tapered bronze/gold crescents, doubled forward chevrons and rising diamond sparks. Initial tube crescents were replaced with broad tapered solid strips to approach the sheet's swept silhouettes. |
| [Slow](enemy-slow-v1.png) | Twelve blue ground squares. | Fractured slate octagon with cyan edge inlays, eight inward frost teeth, four faceted ankle stones, counterturning open shackle bands and restrained blue grit. Its open center preserves the enemy silhouette. |
| [Stoneguard](stoneguard-v1.png) | Four simple grey shield blocks. | Four orbiting pointed slate shields with separate face, bevel, bronze clamps, luminous diamond runes, reinforced rear ribs and stone chips. Low connecting rune remains on the ground. |
| [Thunder Rune](thunder-rune-v1.png) | Thirty-two violet squares expanding for the burst. | Nested slate/ violet ground seal, bronze sockets, radial angular fractures, forked descending lightning with a white core, and outward-moving rubble. Finite lightning and debris animation use the existing short gameplay burst. |
| [Runic Barrier](runic-barrier-v1.png) | Nine plain cyan columns and three front bars. | Four interlocked slate pillars in a compact one-square footprint, alternating heights, chamfered caps and feet, bronze collars/clasps, recessed cyan runes on all sides and fine masonry cracks. Caps were widened and rotated to cover pillar corners. |
| [Mending Rune](mending-rune-v1.png) | Twelve green ground squares and two upright bars. | Four broad slate chevrons join through bright seams into an open diamond knot; gold-bordered green crescents and ascending life diamonds flank the target. Thin tube forms were replaced with actual plates and crescent blades. Life motes stop while damage pauses healing. |
| [Rune of Reckoning](rune-of-reckoning-v1.png) | Twelve orange ground squares. | Open double red judgment diamond, forked central brand, four copper T-prongs with ember slits, radial fractures and ember diamonds. Brackets and hue were refined against the sheet's red/copper construction. |
| [Call to Arms](call-to-arms-v1.png) | Thirty-two amber squares with a small X. | Broad shallow segmented slate annulus at the actual rally radius, bronze/amber perimeter trims, eight ground runes, four compass diamonds, four low rune standards and actual crossed bearded axe silhouettes. Ring width and central axe scale were increased after direct concept comparison. |

The [original renderer](../../src/view/spells-baseline.ts) retains its starting geometry and a frozen copy of its original palette/effect mapping. Original summons are intentionally empty because no dedicated effect existed. Its public preview wrapper is used with the gallery's independent baseline material adapter. Spider-web and spore-cloud incidental effects, and the legacy debug Summon Miner, preserve their original appearance.

The first integrated front/rear/three-quarter captures in `test-results/arcana-gallery-first/` were reviewed spell by spell. They exposed light-clipped rune colors, partly hidden Mending crescents, Thunder's missing central lightning glyph and a rally center that resembled small folded plates. A subsequent iteration separates subdued lit diffuse color from saturated emission, narrows rune strokes, widens Mending's crescents and lights both faces, adds Thunder's slate diamond/zigzag and broken jagged perimeter, and enlarges/mirrors the rally's bearded axe heads. The four rally standards now have a solid octagonal silhouette.

All nine updated close views in `test-results/arcana-gallery-final/` were compared with their concepts. Their silhouette and color corrections read clearly. That review exposed a final Thunder defect: its opaque violet tube enclosed the smaller white core. A single visible pale main strike now carries the flash, with violet branches and perimeter retained. Loose stone fragments for Thunder and Create Stonehand use a restrained untextured cool grey so individual facets remain legible instead of reading almost black.

[Current models](../../src/view/spell-models.ts), [procedural geometry](../../src/view/spell-geometry.ts) and [live effect placement](../../src/view/spells.ts) share the same preview factories as the permanent comparison gallery. Animation is deterministic from simulation time, freezes with pause, and becomes a steady readable pose in reduced motion. Meshes have no picking, text, timers or bars and never alter movement or spell balance. Ground seals remain shallow; concept drawings exaggerate stone thickness and scale for readability. Microfractures, volumetric glow and unbounded lightning are simplified for the overhead camera.

The integrated comparison room and final executable/browser verification are recorded in the [overhaul record](../../arcana-overhaul.md).
