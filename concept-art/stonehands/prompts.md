## Exact prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Create a polished landscape concept-art sheet for STONEHANDS, a proposed expendable magical labor construct in an original stylized 3D dwarven underground stronghold game.
Visual direction: chunky sculpted 3D game-character forms, matte hand-painted surfaces, readable silhouettes, warm beige studio background with soft contact shadows. Dwarven craftsmanship expressed in simple geometric stone cutting and restrained dark bronze joints. Slate-grey stone, worn ochre mineral edges, a small warm amber glowing rune core, subtle amber light in the joints. Avoid photorealism.
Subject: a small, squat, clearly artificial labor golem assembled from only a few roughly hewn stone blocks. Broad oversized three-finger stone hands, short sturdy legs, compact trapezoid torso, tiny inset featureless head with a single recessed horizontal amber eye slit. No mouth, nose, beard, flesh, clothing or dwarf anatomy. One simple geometric glowing rune inset into the chest. Workmanlike, endearing through posture but obviously replaceable equipment, not a heroic living character. Chipped stone edges suggest fragility and cheap replaceable parts. About waist-height to an adult dwarf. Keep details readable from a steep overhead game camera.
Functional design: both hands can grip ordinary tools or carry objects. Carries a plain short-handled iron pickaxe; a modest detachable bronze-framed stone cargo tray fits the upper back. No weapons or combat armor. No huge crystal growths, elaborate steampunk machinery, pipes, engine or steam. Its rune core powers continuous labor without food or sleep.
Sheet composition: a large full-body front three-quarter hero view at left, smaller consistent rear three-quarter view upper middle, smaller steep overhead gameplay view upper right; two small action studies along the bottom right show the SAME Stonehand striking a square gold-veined rock face with its pickaxe, and carrying several gold chunks in its back tray with hands helping balance the load. Show the same proportions, construction and rune core across every view. In the mining vignette, one flat floor meets a full-height squared terrain bank; no stacked playable floors. The gold is embedded in rock, not a mountain of coins. Keep plenty of clean negative space and complete framing.
Exact text only: title "STONEHANDS"; labels "FRONT", "BACK", "GAME VIEW", "MINING", "HAULING". Elegant dark serif title matching a fantasy art-development sheet. No other text, no UI, no health bars, no watermarks, no franchise references.
Produce one high-quality concept sheet, with a charming utilitarian silhouette and convincingly weighty small stone parts.
```

## Review

Inspected the full sheet: coherent stone/bronze design, amber rune core, complete framing, readable front/back/overhead views and mining/hauling poses. This first proposal uses articulated joints and a back tray; these details remain subject to user review.
