# Hearthstone references and model review

The starting Stone Hearth already has an approved [individual concept](../rooms/stone-hearth-v2.png). Its grounded blue crystal cluster, low segmented cradle and bronze/runic circuit remain the target, informed by the newer [stronghold cohesion concept](../terrain/stronghold-cohesion-v2.png). The room footprints pictured in the older sheet are examples, not fixed game templates.

The separate onward relay lacked its own sheet. [onward-hearthstone-v1.png](onward-hearthstone-v1.png) was generated for this project with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-09-09; [the exact prompt](onward-hearthstone-v1-prompt.md) is retained. It shows the same compact stone, bronze and sapphire geometry dormant, awakening and ready. It is a concept reference, not a runtime texture.

The main Hearth now has a low carved stone apron with radial joints, double bronze circuit, twelve open angular glyphs, individually cut protective stones, bronze bindings/clamps and an irregular grounded cluster of blue terminated crystals. Broad colored facets, subordinate crystals and scattered mineral fragments follow the concepts; integral sapphire emission retains dark faces and sharp tips. Damage dims the crystals and runes and exposes subtle stone fractures. The treasury remains managed by the existing furnishing system.

The onward model reduces the same architecture to its existing single reserved tile: layered octagonal stone, eight cut cradle sectors, four brass clamps, one main and three lesser crystals, open glyph inlays and a split compass motif. Normal discovery creates the model; it remains absent while concealed. Actual activation progress illuminates its rune circuit progressively, then the ready state holds a brighter blue core. No floating text, bars or numbers are added. Reduced motion removes the subtle light pulse while preserving all functional state changes.

Starting models are preserved in [hearth-main-baseline.ts](../../src/view/hearth-main-baseline.ts) and [hearth-baseline.ts](../../src/view/hearth-baseline.ts), with frozen [crystal/cradle helpers](../../src/view/arcana-environment-baseline.ts) and isolated reference materials. [The shared refined factory](../../src/view/hearth-models.ts) powers both normal play and the permanent paired comparison room. Animated materials belong to each Hearth instance so gallery damage/activation cannot recolor another exhibit or the settlement core.

Fine concept chipping, refractive interiors and mineral hairline veins are approximated by faceted geometry and deterministic procedural surface detail. The model is solid and deliberately readable at the overhead camera; it does not use expensive transparency, floating shards or new light sources. Browser review evidence is recorded in the overhaul record after inspection.

The first paired review in `test-results/arcana-gallery-first/` found that blue emission flattened the crystal surfaces. The second pass adds broad dark internal mineral planes, branching pale veins and a cyan base gradient; the same shared map modulates emission so it preserves the mineral pattern. Facet contrast was increased, the main core's circuit shifted to the approved sheet's incised gold, and the onward relay's turquoise rune emission was restrained. Geometry and all activation/health sampling remain unchanged by this material refinement.

Refined-only close views in `test-results/arcana-gallery-final/` then exposed repetition of the luminous map at each vertical crystal segment. Continuous bottom-to-tip UVs replace that repetition, retaining one grounded luminous base and a continuous mineral pattern through the broad faces and point. The two Hearth close views in `test-results/arcana-gallery-polish/` confirm that the repeated luminous belts are gone, while the darker mineral planes, pale veins and blue/cyan cluster remain readable. No further model or material changes followed that review.
