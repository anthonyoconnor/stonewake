# Room and structure concept prompts

Cleanup note: superseded concept images and drafts have been removed from the project. This document retains the original prompt wording and source filenames as generation history; references marked removed are not available image files. See the [current gallery](README.md) for the retained concepts.

Historical generation record. The seven expandable room sheets have been revised to replace isolated bedrock mounds with continuous seams that follow the terrain grid. See the [current gallery](README.md) and [correction prompts](prompts-v2.md). Stone Hearth and Bridge v1 remain current. Original prompt wording below is preserved for provenance, not as the current terrain specification.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Each section preserves the exact prompt and reference inputs for one concept sheet. The [gallery](README.md) explains the current set; [Rooms](../../rooms.md) remains the gameplay specification.

The original style-comparison sheet was inspected before use, and only its stylized 3D quadrant was used as a style reference. The Library sheet was generated and visually inspected first, then used as an additional presentation reference for the other sheets. The current room revisions, Stone Hearth, and Bridge images are saved in this folder; superseded room sheets have been removed.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: room environment concept sheet for an original dwarf underground stronghold management game.
Art direction: polished stylized 3D with chunky carved stone, broad readable furniture silhouettes, restrained faceting, softly painted materials, warm practical lights and subtle cool cavern fill. Match ONLY the upper-left "A - Stylized 3D" quadrant of the supplied style-comparison reference; ignore its other art styles and its old room roster. Create a new room sheet, not a style comparison.
Presentation: landscape 3:2 art board with FOUR well-separated overhead three-quarter cutaway dioramas in a clean 2-by-2 arrangement. Neutral dark warm-gray background, unobtrusive typography, enough margins, nothing cropped. Furniture has constant physical scale and appears only where its footprint and a clear usable approach fit. Every study needs a visibly open entrance and circulation routes; doorways, bed or workstation access, narrow arms and routes around bedrock must be clear. Large objects must not float, intersect walls, or seal passages.
The four panels are alternative size/shape examples of ONE room type: "COMPACT" is a small excavated room with a few compact functional furnishings; "EXPANDED" is a larger broad room with repeated or larger furnishing arrangements; "L-SHAPED" is a clearly concave L-shaped room with a narrow arm kept largely clear; "AROUND BEDROCK" is an irregular or U-shaped excavation wrapping an intact dark craggy bedrock mass, with the same designated room floor connecting around it. The last panel has an open cavern edge on one side so its floor identity works without enclosing walls.
Always repeat the SAME unique floor motif and wall treatment across all four variants, including empty designated tiles. Add objects as usable area grows, not by stretching furniture. These are illustrative layouts, not room upgrades or mandatory dimensions.
Text: one room title at top and the exact four panel labels above. No other text, dimensions, stats, arrows, UI, upgrade levels, or capacity claims.
Avoid: photorealism, voxel cubes, pixel art, isometric pixel sprites, overly tiny clutter, heroic statues, tall obstructing walls, Dungeon Keeper copied assets, franchise logos, watermarks.
Room title: "LIBRARY".
This shared stronghold facility attracts Runesmiths who research spells. Room identity: desaturated blue-gray square stone tiles, simple luminous cyan book/rune inlays on occasional floor tiles and a continuous blue border; wall faces have short dark-wood bookshelves, carved script and warm reading lamps.
COMPACT: short wall shelves, one compact lectern/research position and room to approach it; no freestanding shelf rows.
EXPANDED: several research tables and parallel rows of freestanding wooden bookshelves with generous continuous aisles, plus clear circulation from the entry. The room is for working scholars, not a cathedral.
L-SHAPED: compact shelves along the narrow wing, a few floor tiles intentionally empty for circulation, research desks only in the wider part.
AROUND BEDROCK: shelves and workstations fit the available outer walls, desks on the wider side, open blue-tiled route wrapping the bedrock mass and continuing to the cavern edge. Do not put shelves inside bedrock or use a rectangular room hidden beneath the rock.
Readable bound books and modest rune tablets. No throne, altar, shrine, forge, smithing tools, weapon enchanting table, floating spell explosion, beds or separate Priest. Avoid characters in this sheet so the layouts and room architecture remain the focus.
```
