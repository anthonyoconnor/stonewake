# M42 crossing and mineral concepts

Concepts generated and inspected before each map's implementation. Use the three-bank confluence, central radial cave and lava-ring citadel compositions respectively. Incidental stair-like bridge approaches or raised courts in the images do not change the single common floor plane. Bridge decks remain level; chasms remain unbridgeable; scenic crystals do not automatically become income gems. The images guide geography, local clusters and ruin placement, not free room services or new mechanics.

## emberwater

Built-in image generation. Input: `m35-campaign-direction-v1.png` (style only). Output: `m42-emberwater-v1.png`.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: M42 Emberwater Crossing individual level direction, isometric single-layer dwarf dungeon game. Style reference is the game's campaign direction sheet. Create one polished wide isometric view of a COLD RIVER AND MOLTEN STREAM CONFLUENCE underground. A broad irregular teal river separates a west starting bank from a substantial middle rock-and-ruin bank; a winding orange lava branch separates this middle bank from the eastern lost relay district. The channels widen into different pools and taper at several crossing sites, not two straight parallel stripes. Show a small warm Hearth on the western diggable bank, broad playable room-size land shelves, a short northern pair of low stone bridges on an exposed direct route, and a longer southern crossing into a ruined foundry/waystation on a safer middle-bank peninsula. Eastern royal masonry meets natural dark basalt and a small optional crystal recess. Finite gold seams embedded in starter bank earth. Varied widths and irregular shores make the THREE banks and tactical choices obvious. Sparse damp-edge details beside water, scorched rubble near lava, no blanket decoration. All land and bridge decks at exactly one common floor level; uniform full-height square-cell terrain walls. No stairs, no elevation tiers, no overpasses, no giant spires, no large cast actors, no UI, no labels, no watermark. Emphasize practical readable geography and material contrast over spectacle.
```

Create one polished wide isometric RADIAL CRYSTAL CAVERN complex with a CENTRAL small warm Hearth refuge, broad excavatable surrounding earth, and five irregular room-scale cave lobes connected by different-width natural throats around short thick rock ribs. The northeastern lobe holds an old relay; a direct exposed eastward mineral branch and an alternate northern masonry archive circuit approach it from different directions. ```

## ashen-caldera

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: M42 Ashen Caldera individual standalone concept, single-floor isometric dwarf dungeon game. Reference supplies shared campaign style. Create one polished wide isometric dungeon environment organized around a BROKEN RING OF LAVA enclosing a substantial ruined stone citadel island at its center. The starting Hearth refuge lies on a broad WESTERN dry bank outside the ring, with practical excavatable room space and finite gold seams. The onward relay is WITHIN the central citadel, not on the far exterior bank. Show a SHORT EXPOSED western radial bridge approach into a defended gate court, and a LONGER SOUTHERN radial crossing from an abandoned foundry shore into the citadel's service side. Actual walls are uniform full-height square-cell blocks, all floor one plane. No stairs, no raised throne platforms, no multiple levels, no enormous decorative spires, no labels, no UI, no floating text, no watermark. Readable strategy-game composition with generous clear traversable floor.
