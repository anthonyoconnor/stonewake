# M40 Royal Deep concept

Created before authoring the replacement map, using the built-in image-generation tool. The M35 campaign-direction sheet is a visual style reference, not a layout to copy. No production geometry or extra gameplay systems are implied.

## Exact prompt

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one landscape environment concept for the Royal Deep campaign level of a stylized 3D dwarven dungeon-building browser game.
Input image: m35-campaign-direction-v1.png is a STYLE REFERENCE only. Match its material language, overhead camera, substantial square earth/bedrock excavation banks, weathered stone, bronze details and restrained localized light. Create one unified new Royal Deep scene, not a collage.
Primary request: a much broader ancient royal cavern arranged around a large winding lava basin. The west bank has a sheltered rough-earth settlement approach. To the east, monumental ruined royal districts occupy three uneven peninsulas that project into the lava. An exposed short stone bridge at a narrow upper neck leads directly into a defended civic avenue. A distinctly longer bridge farther south reaches an abandoned foundry/service court and provides another land approach around the royal precinct. The final small blue crystalline onward Hearthstone sits in a ruined ceremonial court beyond the peninsulas. An optional gem-mining recess branches away from the required route.
Composition: wide overhead three-quarter view with clear, varied cavern silhouettes and substantial useful room-building ground on both banks. The lava forms a broad irregular basin with narrow branching feeders and outlets, not a straight vertical stripe. The separate crossing sites and their distinct destination districts must read at a glance. Show a few natural outcrops that interrupt the masonry rhythm, dark scorched halls near lava, and warmer surviving bronze wall lamps in occupied remnants. Decorative props are sparse; open floor is important. Use a few small dwarven figures only for scale.
Terrain invariants: ONE terrain layer, ONE common walkable floor height, ONE common unexcavated bank top. All land and all bridge decks are on the SAME floor plane. Water/lava surfaces may sit just below the banks. No stairs, terraces, raised platforms, stacked levels, floor elevations, or bridges over other roads. Ruined pilasters are shallow attachments to existing full-height bank walls; do not turn land into tiny raised islands. Lava glows orange against charcoal stone; the Hearthstone remains a small blue crystal, never a fire pit.
Avoid: labels, headings, UI, floating text, numbers, progress bars, elaborate furniture everywhere, a full-map border of empty rock, perfectly symmetrical geography, and copying any layout in the reference. This is a single coherent environment illustration to guide the level's feel.

## Interpretation

The authored map will keep the shared simulation and common floor plane. Any incidental concept architecture is decorative reference only. The two crossings must be constructed and paid for in play; the concept's completed bridges communicate their intended locations rather than granting starting bridges.
