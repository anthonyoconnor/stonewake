# Dwarf concept art prompts

Cleanup note: superseded concept images and drafts have been removed from the project. This document retains the original prompt wording and source filenames as generation history; references marked removed are not available image files. See the [current gallery](README.md) for the retained concepts.

Historical record of the original concept set. Some prompts describe roles and facilities that have since been merged or removed. Their wording is preserved as generation provenance, not current game rules. See the [current gallery](README.md) and [revision prompts](prompts-v2.md) for the simplified roster.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. These are visual concept studies, not rigged models or finalized character identities.

Each sheet shows a front three-quarter view, a rear view, and an overhead readability view. The miner establishes the common art direction; the other sheets use miner-v1.png as their visual reference. Clothing, gender, palettes, and equipment appearance are proposals for discussion, not additions to game mechanics.

## Miner

Reference: none (new image).

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production-minded character concept sheet for the MINER in an original dwarf underground stronghold management game. This is a raster concept illustration, not a finished game model. Art direction: polished stylized 3D, chunky sculpted forms, restrained faceting, broad readable shapes, softly painted material variation, matte leather and cloth, worn forged metal, grounded adult fantasy proportions. Short, exceptionally broad dwarf about three-and-a-half heads tall with big hands, sturdy boots, substantial copper beard gathered into two simple sections, strong nose and a practical friendly face. Silhouette must read from the rotating overhead gameplay camera. Clothing: simple ochre work tunic, charcoal trousers, brown leather harness, gloves and reinforced boots; squat battered iron mining helmet with a small protected amber lamp. Main identifying object: a chunky practical pickaxe carried low in one hand, not raised to fight. A compact gold-hauling satchel at the hip and modest tool belt support the role; avoid excessive tiny accessories. This is an affordable civilian excavator, gold collector and wall-reinforcement worker, not an armored warrior. Composition: landscape 3:2 professional concept sheet on a clean warm-gray studio background. At left, one large full-body front three-quarter view showing complete head and both boots. At upper right, a smaller full-body rear three-quarter view of the EXACT SAME design and proportions, showing harness and satchel. At lower right, a compact full-body view of the SAME dwarf from a steep 60-degree overhead gameplay angle that tests readability. Leave generous space between views and do not crop tools. Consistent warm soft key lighting and subtle ground contact shadows in all views. Clear small typography: title 'MINER' at top left; view labels 'FRONT', 'BACK', 'GAME VIEW' under the respective views. No other text. No scenery, no platforms, no room, no grid, no stat cards, no logos, no existing franchise design. No photorealism, voxel cubes, pixel art, line drawing, baby proportions, giant glowing weapons, exaggerated armor, or excessive greebles. The three views depict one coherent dwarf from different directions.
```

## SMITH

```text

Smith: a powerful broad-shouldered adult dwarf woman with a square friendly face, warm copper-brown skin, thick dark-auburn hair in two short heavy braids pulled away from her face, soot on cheeks, no beard. Her silhouette is a broad uninterrupted dark-brown leather smithing apron, rolled rust-red sleeves, thick heat gloves and heavy boots. Bare head with a simple leather hair tie. Carries a short square-headed forging hammer low in one hand and a pair of sturdy blacksmith tongs in the other; both are working tools, not oversized combat weapons. Simple reinforced leather shoulder pad, no suit of armor. Rear view shows crossed apron straps. Equipment role is making weapons and armor for others. Title 'SMITH'.
